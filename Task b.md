## Part C — 3 Recommendations for Earning AI-Search Citations

1. **Get listed and reviewed on third-party B2B directories** (Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush) rather than relying only on owned-site claims — AI answer engines lean heavily on independently-verifiable sources over first-party marketing pages, since they're triangulating trust signals the model can cross-check.

2. **Publish answer-first, structured content** — pages and articles that state a direct answer in the first sentence, use clear H2/H3 questions, and include FAQ schema. AI engines tend to extract and cite content that's already packaged as a clean, quotable answer rather than long-form narrative copy.

3. **Earn mentions in independent "best of" roundups and community discussions** (industry blogs, Reddit, niche newsletters) — citations in AI answers often trace back to a brand being repeatedly mentioned across multiple independent sources, not just once on the brand's own site.

**Entities to cover:** Shopify Plus, WooCommerce, WordPress, hosting providers, PCI compliance, checkout, apps/plugins, themes, POS, multi-currency.

**FAQ (4 questions, answer-first style):**
1. **Is Shopify or WooCommerce cheaper to run long-term?** Shopify has a predictable monthly cost with hosting included; WooCommerce is free but requires separate hosting, security, and maintenance costs that often add up to a similar or higher total.
2. **Can I switch from WooCommerce to Shopify later?** Yes — migration is common and typically involves moving product data, redirecting old URLs, and rebuilding the theme, usually over 4-8 weeks depending on store size.
3. **Which platform is better for SEO?** Both can rank well; WooCommerce (on WordPress) offers more native content flexibility, while Shopify has improved significantly with built-in speed and structured data support.
4. **Do I need a developer for either platform?** Basic stores on both can be self-managed; Shopify Plus and heavily customized WooCommerce builds typically need developer support.
